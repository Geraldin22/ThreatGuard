Security Tips

Protect Against Threats

Habit

Think Before You Click

Check links and messages carefully before opening or responding.

Hover over a link to preview where it really goes. If the address looks odd or shortened, do not open it.

Identity

Verify the Sender

Make sure the message is from a trusted person or organization.

Contact the company through its official app or website, not through the number or link in the message.

Privacy

Protect Personal Information

Never share passwords, OTPs, or sensitive details through suspicious messages.

Real banks and services never ask for your password or one-time code by text or email.

Response

Report Suspicious Activity

Report phishing attempts or unusual messages to help prevent further threats.

Forward scams to your provider or local cyber-crime unit, then delete the message.

Install ThreatGuard on your phone

Your browser did not offer a one-tap install, so use the steps below.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open this page in Chrome, not inside Messenger, Facebook or another app.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu at the top right.
  3. Tap Install app or Add to Home screen, then confirm.

iPhone (Safari)

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.

After installing on Android, Share → ThreatGuard appears when you long-press a message in your SMS or chat app.
